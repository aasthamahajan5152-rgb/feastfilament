FeastFilament
India generates over 87,000 tonnes of food waste daily, most of which ends up in landfills, releasing methane and worsening climate change. Our venture tackles this problem by transforming food waste into eco-friendly 3D printing filament, promoting a circular economy where waste becomes a resource. By merging sustainability with innovation, we enable industries, makers, and students to print responsibly—reducing carbon footprint while driving a greener future.
